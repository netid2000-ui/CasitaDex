# Designs by FA — professional standards and knowledge index

Owner approved November 25, 2026. Applies to Event Command by F&A and CasitaDex. This is the versioned operating standard derived from the completed 14-discipline, 2,200-new-hostname research program. The source corpus uses normalized hostnames, may include different subdomains from one publisher, and reviews readable excerpts, not entire courses or publications.

**Canonical reference:** [research corpus, pinned checkpoint](https://github.com/netid2000-ui/event-command-by-fa/tree/1933e4a9ae5323c1a59b77f9876e3f17a746f925/docs/research) and [final findings](https://github.com/netid2000-ui/event-command-by-fa/blob/1933e4a9ae5323c1a59b77f9876e3f17a746f925/docs/research/professional-research-final-five-20261125.md). Use authorized access. Research informs decisions; current owner-approved decisions and actual validated evidence govern implementation. For new recommendations, link the relevant source(s) and compare with existing capabilities. Do not blindly adopt other products' pricing, design or technical practices.

## Decision protocol

1. Capture the user's outcome, relevant owner decisions, approved source and Figma baseline, applicable FA IDs, and relevant source links. For changed facts read current authoritative docs.
2. Translate findings into a small, testable change; include origin, exit, failure and recovery states, source paths, acceptance criteria and privacy/cost impact. Protect existing user data and released/accepted artifacts.
3. Use existing tools first. Record the installed version and actual environment on the designated Mac mini. Start with local source checks, then simulator and emulator, Samsung and owner iPhone. Validate exact current build identity before native testing.
4. Reconcile approved Figma node/component/variant with native captures; correct verified mismatches within scope and rerun affected flows. Never call inferred frame matches exact matches.
5. Record evidence: source SHA and status; build identity and platform; test fixture; expected vs observed; status PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED/SKIPPED/NOT_RUN; raw local evidence link; Figma node; disposition and owner decision. Historical evidence is labeled with its original date.
6. Do not claim release readiness until all required current candidate checks and owner sign-off are confirmed. No production deploy, release merge, store submission, live purchase or new paid service without the owner.

## Operating priorities

P0: baseline provenance, end-to-end Save/Cancel/Back/return routes; local durability and restore; keyboard, accessibility and both themes; event-size fixtures and household retrieval; cost and release gates. P1: complete exact Figma-control mapping and automated fixture coverage; assistive technologies; offline negative paths; entitlement sandbox. P2: optional new tools only after a measured gap. Any proposed product scope change goes to the owner rather than silently entering an accepted design.

## Evidence status vocabulary

- PASS: an executed assertion produced the expected outcome on the recorded build/environment.
- FAIL: executed assertion differed from expected; preserve evidence and retest correction.
- BLOCKED: execution prerequisite unavailable, with blocker and recovery.
- SKIPPED: explicitly omitted by the suite or owner, with reason.
- NOT_RUN: no fresh execution. Prior evidence can be cited but is not a new pass.
