# SB-18 — Moderated Household Memory UX Research Protocol
Date: 2026-10-15. Status: READY FOR RECRUITING, NOT CONDUCTED.

## Questions to answer
1. Can a homeowner save a genuinely useful answer without a taxonomy/inventory setup?
2. Do users naturally search by problem, object, place or need?
3. Is “Home · Find · Add · More” with Spaces inside Find easier than “Home · Find · Add · Spaces · More”? A/B test both, counterbalance order; do not treat designer choice as user evidence.
4. Are photo and voice capture meaningful aids or merely distracting features?
5. What signals make a saved answer trustworthy: location, last verification date, source, image, simple “Needs checking” status?
6. Is the difference between Free manual backup, paid Cloud Care automation, and provider-supplied storage clear?
7. Can people complete core tasks without ever seeing a paywall or feeling pressured to build an inventory?

## Proposed research sample
10–12 moderated participants, selected with informed opt-in: include people with a single residence and those with multiple spaces, renters/homeowners, caregivers, strong and weak technical confidence, users of OneDrive/iCloud/Drive, and 2–3 users with larger text/screen reader needs. Sample is qualitative and cannot prove population demand or pricing conversion. No recording without explicit consent; do not collect participants' real home secrets, addresses, codes or identifying photos.

## Tasks (unassisted)
- First launch: save the size of a hypothetical HVAC filter. Observe time, steps, hesitation.
- Retrieve saved filter while “at the hardware store” using an approximate phrase. Observe whether the exact size is apparent in results.
- Photograph a fictional model label: locate camera pathway; observe comprehension of a preview-only prototype and permission rationale.
- Capture a hypothetical voice instruction without transcription; interpret what will be searchable offline.
- Locate water shutoff information and tell whether it has been verified.
- Find a saved item by location or room without completing room categorization beforehand.
- Discover and use Free manual backup in a simulated setting. Ask what would happen after device failure.
- Evaluate optional Cloud Care: explain whose cloud quota is used, costs, what happens if the subscription expires.
- First-run response to upsell absence, clarity of “Saved on this device,” language warmth and confidence.
- Accessibility variation: 1.3x and 2x large text, screen reader, buttons/contrast, interrupted save, landscape/foldable.

## Balanced experiments
A/B test four destinations (Home, Find, Add, More) versus five with Spaces. Compare answer-first text cards with photo-first thumbnails; text-first Add versus capture-choice Add. Alternate which variant is shown first; keep same realistic tasks. Do not ask “Which is prettier?” until after successful task trials.
Price tests are separate and do NOT precede open-ended need discussions. Show fully disclosed pricing cards, including Microsoft/Google/Apple separate storage costs and recurrence; ask commitment intent only with limitations clearly recorded.

## Recording template
Participant alias, segment, task, variant order, completed Y/N, taps, elapsed time, wrong paths, error recovery, spontaneous quotes, confusion moments, accessibility adjustments, trust comprehension, question confidence. Keep individual notes free of confidential household content. Summarize counts with denominators, ranges, and dissenting observations; no invented statistical significance.

## Design-revision rule
One participant's preference is a signal, not a product mandate. Fix severe core-task failure promptly. Preserve original tested prototypes and evidence. Publish study findings, actionable changes, which hypothesis each change addresses, and a repeated validation plan before final owner design signoff.

## Stop conditions
Research does not authorize paid cloud or Production. Do not deploy analytics, install third-party SDKs, recruit actual participants without appropriate privacy/consent preparations or collect live personal household information through the prototype.
