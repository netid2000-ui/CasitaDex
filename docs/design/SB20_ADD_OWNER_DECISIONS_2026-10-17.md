# CasitaDex SB-20 Add tab — owner design decisions
Date 2026-10-17; owner Designs by F&A.
DESIGN ONLY. Implementation, schema/data migrations, commits, builds and deployment remain on hold.
References: Home v1.1 signed off; Find and Answer Detail v0.9 signed off.

## DECISION 1 APPROVED — Quick save with optional sections, plus Quick Photo
Owner chose Add Option B: title and answer in the main quick-save form; organizing information and other supporting fields in optional sections. ADD AN IMMEDIATELY VISIBLE QUICK PHOTO entry point rather than burying photo capture in optional detail sections. User wants to capture and save photographs as rapid visual memory references, including taking a picture or choosing one already on device. Photo-first vs mandatory text, camera/library entry wording, source/location of file, image safety/permission/storage, media lifecycle and backup/restore constraints are design refinements still to be approved. No automatic image interpretation, fake OCR or AI recognition assumed. Do not modify running application code.

## DECISION 2 APPROVED — Title plus written answer OR photo
Owner approved Quick Photo Option B: meaningful title is required; a nonempty saved written answer OR at least one valid locally saved photo satisfies memory content requirement, and both may be supplied. Take Photo and Choose Photo are immediately visible in Add. Photo-only memories must remain searchable from their human title and optional Space/Categories/notes, and never display fabricated photo-derived text or claim OCR/AI interpretation/verification. Preview, safe import, failure handling, attachments, draft recovery, backup/restore and lifecycle require detailed design and later QA. Source Card currently requires answer string; schema and validation changes will need backward-compatible engineering design before implementation. Existing user content must be preserved. No application code, migration, build, commit, or deployment authorized.

## DECISION 3 APPROVED — Title first, Quick Photo and answer together
Owner approved Option B. On Add, the meaningful required title appears first; Take photo, Choose photo and an optional written-answer field appear together directly below it, with neither media capture option hidden in an accordion. Organize and More details remain optional expandable sections. Save requires title AND (meaningful written answer OR at least one valid, successfully imported photo). Photo-only record must have truthful thumbnail presentation in Find/Detail and searchable user-authored title/optional metadata; no inferred OCR or image description. Quick Photo limits, durable draft recovery, duplicate suggestions and acceptance thresholds still require owner design review. No app implementation authorized.

## DECISION 4 APPROVED — Up to five photos per memory
Owner selected Option B: a saved memory may contain zero to five photos (photo-only is permitted with meaningful required title, per Decision 2). Take/Choose photo remain visible after title. Up to five should be enforced before attempting an over-limit attachment, with user-friendly explanation; existing legitimate records with more photos must not be truncated or deleted during migration. Prioritize source-image label legibility and full-size inspection. Final image encoding/size thresholds require real-device testing and local storage checks; never claim backup success solely from local import. Draft recovery, duplicate checks, media lifecycle, 30-day Deleted recovery and app storage-pressure safeguards are design/QA gates; no app code, build, commit or migration authorized.

## DECISION 5 APPROVED — Single recoverable draft
Owner selected Option B: one locally autosaved unfinished Add draft, recoverable after interruption. Returning offers Continue or Discard, never silently overwrites the draft. Stage imported photos safely and retain existing saved user content on failure; clear draft only after durable record plus media save; draft is not searchable in Find. Exact autosave cadence and recovery mechanics require engineering tests. No app implementation authorized.

## PHOTO IMPORT APPROVED — Option B: library and image-file browsing
Owner selected Option B: on Add, Choose/Import Photo permits selecting existing images from the device photo library AND browsing image files via native system picker. Separately approved Take Photo remains visible for camera capture. Imported media must be copied into app-managed local draft storage, validated, displayed in preview, and durably committed before Save reports success. Up to five photos per new memory; meaningful title plus answer or valid photo required. Cancel/denied permissions/errors preserve one recoverable local draft. No Share-to-CasitaDex extension was approved. Native Android handling, media limits, backups, deletion lifecycle and accessibility require subsequent engineering validation. Implementation remains ON HOLD.
