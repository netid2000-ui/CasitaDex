5. QA: targeted tests after each meaningful module change; one integrated test/typecheck/lint pass after convergence; one native build after source stabilizes. Rerun only failed or affected tests after fixes.

7. STORE AUDIT: policy-to-evidence matrix, SDK/privacy/permission inventory, Apple/Google listing assets and release gates. Source and date official policy references. No assumption of preapproval.

## Tool/credit budget controls
- Prefer AI Commander on primary host; batch related reads and edits, not constant status polling.
- Check status at phase boundaries or after a meaningful wait, not repeated rapid queries.
- Reuse existing caches and build outputs; avoid reinstalling tools or recreating emulator.
- Limit external research to authoritative Apple, Google, Figma resources; reuse results in documented citations.
- On repeated blocker: at most two equivalent retries; then one distinct safe approach, or stop and report.
- No speculative UI alternatives, redundant screenshots, repeated full builds or parallel conflicting agents.
- Do not claim completion without acceptance evidence; preserve dirty owner files, no commits/pushes/merges.
