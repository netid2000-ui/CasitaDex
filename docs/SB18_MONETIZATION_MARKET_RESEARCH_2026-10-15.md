# SB-18 — Monetization Market Research and Competitive Evidence
Research date: October 15, 2026. Owner: Designs by F&A. Status: decision-support research, NOT validated demand / revenue forecast / authority to bill or deploy cloud. U.S. published pricing, potentially subject to plan, storefront, and promotions.

## Product constraints
CasitaDex V1 local-first, one useful answer, offline, privacy, portable user-owned data. No ads, artificial loss of access, blocked accessibility, or forced sign-in. Current charter lifetime Complete launch hypothesis ($14.99-$19.99) is unvalidated; cloud convenience product direction is owner approved in Amendment D, not implementation-authorized. `docs/PRODUCT_CHARTER.md` is authoritative.

## Observed competition (official listings / publisher pages)
| App | Published price / model | Feature signal | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sortly | Free up to 100 unique items; Advanced $24/mo introductory annual-billed, regular higher; larger B2B tiers | QR/barcodes, item properties, team access | High B2B pricing is NOT a consumer price anchor. |
| HomeZada | Free Essentials; $99/yr Premium; $189/yr Deluxe | maintenance, documents, finances, AI, property transfer add-ons | Broad home-management scope justifies higher tiers but risks bloat. |
| Under My Roof | Free 10-item trial, $4.99/mo or $34.99/yr | local/iCloud, photos, voice-assisted entry, receipt capture, warranties, reminders | Directly contests local-first photo/voice/cloud positioning. |
| Tody | Core free, $9.99/yr Premium, household sharing $25-$80/yr | chore reminders, multi-user coordination | Recurring household sync may have standalone value. |
| Itemtopia | $7.99/mo and $79.99/yr listed; user-seat purchases | multi-user inventory/records | Collaboration/permissions can create revenue. |
| Home Contents | Free limited to 25 items; advertised one-time Unlimited unlock | rapid capture, room organization, CSV/export | Permanent unlock is a demonstrated alternative to subscriptions. |
| Nest Egg - Inventory | iOS paid $6.99 plus IAP; advertises no subscription for unlimited items | scan/barcodes, offline-focused inventory | One-time consumer willingness exists, price lower than full SaaS. |

**Not competitor market share or revenue:** app reviews, price lists and store visibility show only offerings, not paid conversion, usage, margin or demand. A competitor feature appearing on a store listing is NOT evidence CasitaDex users want it. We should avoid feature parity as the roadmap.

## Needs and public discussion: directional, not representative samples
- Insurance Information Institute advises photo documentation, receipts and separate/off-site backup; this supports documentation and backup value, NOT claims-adjustment, payout or insurance-compliance guarantees.
- Homeowner Reddit threads describe forgotten maintenance, disjoint notes/binders and reluctance to pay for overly complex upsells; other homeowners say a calendar or paper binder suffices. Reflect both attitudes in user research.
- Existing mobile products (Under My Roof, RoomGenie, StowTap, Trove) advertise AI photo parsing, NFC/QR, receipt scans, and find-by-location. These are crowded feature categories, not unique CasitaDex claims.
- Differentiation to TEST: fewer taps to save/find a specific fact, default offline, plain-language trust state, independent backup, and predictable pricing.
