### 1. **Simplicity and Clarity**
   - **Frame ID:** 1632:651
   - **Issue:** The Add Memory screen is cluttered with too many input fields and options, making it difficult for users to understand what information is required and how to proceed.
   - **Impact:** Users may feel overwhelmed and give up on adding memories, leading to a poor user experience.
   - **Acceptance Check:** Conduct a usability test with a small group of users to see if they can easily add a memory without frustration.

### 2. **Accessibility**
   - **Frame ID:** 1632:651
   - **Issue:** The text fields and buttons are too small, especially the "Add Memory" button, which is only 48x84 pixels. This does not meet the 48dp touch target size requirement for accessibility.
   - **Impact:** Users with motor disabilities may struggle to interact with the app, leading to frustration and potentially dropping the app.
   - **Acceptance Check:** Use an accessibility tool to check if all text fields and buttons meet the 48dp touch target size requirement.

### 3. **Dark Mode**
   - **Frame ID:** 1624:345
   - **Issue:** The dark mode design does not provide sufficient contrast between text and background, which can make it difficult to read.
   - **Impact:** Users may experience eye strain and difficulty in using the app in low-light conditions.
   - **Acceptance Check:** Use a color contrast checker to ensure that all text and background colors meet the WCAG 2.2 contrast requirements.

### 4. **Platform-Specific Material**
   - **Frame ID:** 1679:953 (Android) and 1679:793 (iOS)
   - **Issue:** The design does not follow the platform-specific material guidelines, such as using glass for navigation controls and retaining section state in tab bars.
   - **Impact:** The app may not feel native to the platform, leading to a poor user experience.
   - **Acceptance Check:** Compare the design to the platform-specific material guidelines and make necessary adjustments.

### 5. **Product Differentiation**
   - **Frame ID:** 1693:861
   - **Issue:** The Free plan does not differentiate itself from other similar apps in terms of features and functionality.
   - **Impact:** Users may not see the value in the app and may choose a competitor instead.
   - **Acceptance Check:** Conduct a market research survey to understand how users perceive the app's value proposition and make necessary adjustments.

### 6. **Interaction/Navigation**
   - **Frame ID:** 1679:953 and 1679:793
   - **Issue:** The navigation is inconsistent across different screens, with some screens using a tab bar and others using a list of options.
   - **Impact:** Users may find it difficult to navigate the app and may become frustrated.
   - **Acceptance Check:** Conduct a usability test with a small group of users to see if they can easily navigate the app and make necessary adjustments.
